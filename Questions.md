# DealerPulse — Curated AI Questions

These 20 questions are the ones the DealerPulse team will most want answered from `dealership_data.json`. They are available as **one-click prompts in the AI assistant** (see the AI assistant plan in `PLANNER.md`). The user can also ask any free-form question; the integrated LLM answers it from the computed metrics.

---

## Performance vs. target

1. Which branches are on/behind/ahead of their monthly unit & revenue targets?
2. What is the projected month-end performance (best/expected/worst) vs. target given the open pipeline?
3. How does each branch's delivery rate trend Jun→Dec — improving, flat, or deteriorating?

## Reps & effectiveness

4. Which rep closes the most units and which has the best conversion rate (vs. lead volume)?
5. Which reps are high-volume but low-converting — where is the coaching opportunity?
6. Which reps have the most stale/unfollowed leads, and for how many days?
7. Which reps significantly over- or under-perform their branch peers (statistical anomaly)?

## Funnel & conversion

8. Which funnel stage has the biggest drop-off (new→contacted→test_drive→negotiation→order→delivered)?
9. How long do leads spend in each stage vs. the benchmark — which stages drag?
10. Which lead sources (walk_in, website, referral…) produce the highest conversion and revenue per lead?
11. Which models sell best and which yield the highest deal value / revenue share?

## Loss & risk

12. What are the top reasons leads are lost, and how do they differ by branch?
13. How do lost reasons correlate with source and model (e.g., test-drive dissatisfaction on Fortuner)?
14. Which open leads are at risk of missing their `expected_close_date` given recent activity?
15. Which leads have aged without any follow-up since their last activity?

## Delivery & fulfillment

16. What is the average days-to-deliver per branch, and which branches are slowest?
17. What are the most common delivery-delay causes, and where do they cluster?
18. Which models take longest to deliver — and does that drive lost deals?

## Forecasting & patterns

19. What is the total open pipeline value (weighted by stage) per branch?
20. Which month had the strongest/weakest conversion, and which model/source drove it?
